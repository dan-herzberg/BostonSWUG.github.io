About This Meeting

This meeting kicked off BostonSWUG's partnership with the brand-new Mill Forge Makerspace in Norwood — our first official user group meeting at the space, following an informal grand-opening visit earlier that summer. As always: free SOLIDWORKS training, networking with local engineers, and dinner.
