IRONSWUG

A hands-on engineering challenge meetup, headlined by the K'nex Motorcycle Derby.

Date Friday, June 23, 2023
Recap

About This Meeting

IRONSWUG was a hands-on, engineering-challenge-flavored BostonSWUG meetup — headlined by the K'nex Motorcycle Derby, where small teams designed and built K'nex motorcycles, then raced them down a ramp for distance. It was a direct precursor to the K'nex Bridge Challenge that's since become a Summer Festival tradition.
